Context

The rendered cause table for #4471 contradicts its written “one issue” conclusion. The same subject can describe a shared-parser failure or a booking-v3 hint failure. Browser metadata and intake tags can also disagree with tenant settings.

Proposed decision

Replace decision 0001 when the support desk accepts this proposal. Keep that accepted record unchanged. Match reports by verified cause and affected path, using tenant locale, form version, and channel. A matching subject is a search aid, not proof of duplication. A missing tenant or contradictory evidence requires investigation; it does not justify a merge.

Keep stable report IDs and source links. Deduplicate repeated export rows by ID, while retaining distinct reports with identical wording. Keep received time separate from merge time. Record who reviewed the evidence and revisit unresolved reports before closing a ticket.

Consequences

Triage requires reading and tenant lookup. Engineering receives separate, reproducible failure paths and traceable counts. Existing #4471 merges need review; this proposal does not claim the live desk has adopted the rule. See the incident review.
